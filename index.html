Questions executors ask

Is this really free?

Yes — every feature, no limits, no email required. The project is supported by ads on this page (never inside the tool).

Will the court accept this form?

The report follows the standard charge-and-discharge structure many courts expect. Always confirm your court's required format — some courts have their own forms. This tool organizes and presents; it is not legal advice.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser only (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded. Clearing browser data erases it, so download a JSON backup weekly — we remind you.

What about taxes (Form 706 / 1041)?

The ledger helps organize records for your tax preparer; it does not prepare tax returns.
